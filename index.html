About Me

I am a biomedical engineering student at Department of Biomedical Engineering, University Basel. I received my bachelor's degree in 2023 from West China School/Hospital of Stomatology at Sichuan University (SCU), one of the top dental schools in China.My background spans dentistry, biomaterials, biomedical image processing and analysis.

Currently, I am currently working on my master`s thesis at the Functional and Molecular Imaging Lab, ETH Zurich, under the guidance of Dr. Luis Deán-Ben and superivised by Prof. Daniel Razansky, where I focus on developing model-based and CNN-regularized algorithms for high-resolution photoacoustic image reconstruction.

I completed my semester project at the Multi-Scale Robotics Lab (MSRL), D-MAVT, ETH Zurich, focusing on the development of thermally stimulated self-locking cuff electrodes designed for magnetically assisted neural stimulation. I am fortunate to be advised by Huimin Chen, under the supervision of Prof. Salvador Pané i Vidal and Dr. Semih Sevim.
