About Me

I am a biomedical engineering student at Department of Biomedical Engineering, University Basel. I received my bachelor's degree in 2023 from West China School/Hospital of Stomatology at Sichuan University (SCU), ranked top 15 globally in Dentistry (QS 2025).

Currently, I am currently working on my master`s thesis at the Functional and Molecular Imaging Lab, ETH Zurich, under the guidance of Prof. Dr. Daniel Razansky and Dr. Xose Luis Dean-Ben, where I focus on applying physics-based unrolled deep learning algorithms for optoacoustic tomography (OAT) reconstruction.

I completed my semester project at the Multi-Scale Robotics Lab (MSRL), D-MAVT, ETH Zurich, focusing on the development of thermally stimulated self-locking cuff electrodes designed for magnetically assisted neural stimulation. I am fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Salvador Pané i Vidal, Dr. Semih Sevim and Huimin Chen.
