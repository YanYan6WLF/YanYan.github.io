Master Thesis: Optoacoustic Image Reconstruction

Multiscale Functional and Molecular Imaging Lab, D-MAVT, ETH Zurich
07.2025--Present
Advisors: Dr. Luis Deán-Ben, Prof. Daniel Razansky
  • Performed optoacoustic image reconstruction using homogeneous and heterogeneous back-projection as well as LSQR-based heterogeneous models to reconstruct in vivo mouse abdomen images.
  • Implemented and compared vesselness enhancement filters (Frangi, Jerman, and others) for post-processing.
  • Developed an unrolled, physics-guided deep learning framework that integrates CNN-based image enhancement with model-based reconstruction, where the CNN acts as a self-learned regularizer.
  • Conducted systematic comparison and evaluation of reconstruction performance across different algorithm.

Semester Project: Self-locking Cuff Electrodes for the Nerve Stimulation

Multi-Scale Robotics Laboratory, D-MAVT, ETH Zurich
10.2024--07.2025
Advisors: Huimin Chen, Dr. Semih Sevim, Prof. Salvador Pané i Vidal
Presentation; Report
  • Determined and optimized PEGDA/PNIPAM hydrogel formulations for DLP-based 3D printing.
  • Fabricated PDMS-acrylic pool chambers for hydrogel photocuring via PDMS spin-coating and oxygen-plasma bonding.
  • Designed and 3D-printed bilayer hydrogel cuff electrodes integrating PNIPAM and PEGDA.
  • Controlled curvature deformation under thermal stimulation through structural design optimization.
  • Designed and 3D-printed flow chambers to evaluate cuff-electrode adhesion under fluid conditions.
  • Utilized CAD modeling in Onshape, UV polymerization setup, and optical imaging techniques for characterization.
