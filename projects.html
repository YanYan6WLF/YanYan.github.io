Master Thesis: Optoacoustic Image Reconstruction

Multiscale Functional and Molecular Imaging Lab, D-MAVT, ETH Zurich
07.2025--Present
Advisors: Dr. Luis Deán-Ben
  • Designed a FISTA-inspired reconstruction algorithm for optoacoustic tomography (OAT), where each iteration combines:
    • Nesterov acceleration used to speed up convergence
    • Shared CNN-based self-learned regularizer applied at every iteration
    • Physics-informed data consistency term
  • Building and training the self-learned CNN regularizer using:
    • Weighted dual-speed-of-sound (2SoS) back-projection reconstructions and the first ten 2SoS LSQR reconstruction iterations as inputs
    • Vesselness-enhanced (Frangi, Jerman filters, etc.) images as structural ground truth
  • Will conduct testing and validation across independent OAT acquisitions.
  • May additionally engage in phantom data acquisition, depending on project timeline and availability.

Semester Project: Self-locking Cuff Electrodes for the Nerve Stimulation

Multi-Scale Robotics Laboratory, D-MAVT, ETH Zurich
10.2024--07.2025
Advisors: Huimin Chen, Dr. Semih Sevim, Prof. Salvador Pané i Vidal
Presentation; Report
  • Determined and optimized PEGDA/PNIPAM hydrogel formulations for DLP-based 3D printing
  • Fabricated PDMS pool chambers via PDMS spin-coating and oxygen-plasma treatment for hydrogel photocuring
  • Designed printing patterns on AutoCAD and printed thermally stimulated PNIPAM/PEGDA bilayer hydrogel cuff electrodes
  • Controlled thermally induced curvature deformation through structural design optimization
  • Designed and 3D-printed nerve fixation and sealing connectors in Onshape to construct a microfluidic channel for evaluating cuff-electrode attachment under programmed flow conditions mimicking in vivo environments.
  • Utilized micro-CT and optical imaging techniques for structural and morphological characterization.

04.2024--06.2024
Report
